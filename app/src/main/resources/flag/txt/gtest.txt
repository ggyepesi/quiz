Additionally, the Union Jack features in many territorial and sub-national flags. These are often [[Red Ensign]]s (e.g., {{flagof|Bermuda}}) or [[Blue Ensign]]s (e.g., {{flagof|New South Wales}} and {{flagof|Anguilla}}). A small number have backgrounds of other colors (e.g. {{flagof|the=yes|British Antarctic Territory}} and {{flagof|Niue}}) or a unique pattern in the field (e.g. {{flagof|the=yes|British Indian Ocean Territory}} and {{flagof|Hawaii}}). A small number put the Union Jack somewhere other than the canton (e.g. {{flagof|British Columbia}}). Unofficial flags, such as {{flagof|Ross Dependency|unofficial}} also use it.

*{{flagof|Brazil}} – "Ordem e Progresso", meaning "Order and Progress" in Portuguese.
*{{flagof|Equatorial Guinea}} – "Unidad, Paz, Justicia", meaning "Unity, Peace, Justice" in Spanish
*{{flagof|Iran}} –  the [[Takbir]] (''"Allahu akbar"'', which means "God is [the] greatest") written in the [[Kufic| Kufic script]] 11 times.

===Country name and motto===

*{{flagof|Afghanistan|2013}} (2013–2021) – the lowest line of text reads ''Afghanistan'' in the [[Pashto alphabet]], and the calligraphic text at the top is the [[Shahada]] with the [[Takbir]] written beneath it.
*{{flagof|Brunei}} – the line of text on the crescent reads ''"Always render service with God's guidance"'', while the lower line reads ''Brunei Darussalam'', both in the [[Jawi script]].
*{{flagof|El Salvador}} – the name of the country encircles the coat of arms, which features the motto "Dios, Unión, Libertad" (Spanish for "God, Unity, Freedom") inside.
*{{flagof|the=yes|Dominican Republic}} – the motto "Dios, Patria, Libertad" (meaning "God, Homeland, Freedom" in Spanish) can be read above the coat of arms at the center, below is the name of the country.
{{clear}}

===Other texts===

*{{flagof|California}} – the name of the short lived and unrecognized state "California Republic", which preceded California's admission into the Union.
*{{flagof|the=yes|Dominican Republic}} – the Bible is opened to the Gospel of John, chapter 8, verse 32, which reads “Y la verdad los hará libres”, which translates to “And [[the truth will set you free|the truth shall set you free]]” from Spanish.
*{{flagof|Guatemala}} – "Libertad 15 de septiembre de 1821", a combination of the Spanish word for "Freedom" and the date of independence of the former [[Federal Republic of Central America]] from Spain.
*{{flagof|Haiti}} –  "L'union fait la force" (meaning "Union makes strength" in French), which is different from the country's official motto "Liberté, égalité, fraternité".
*{{flagof|Malta}} – "For Gallantry" can be read at the [[George Cross]] carried in the canton.

== Unique aspect ratio==
<!--List is incomplete. More national flags exist which are uniquely shaped, although just by having uncommon aspect ratios.-->
Most common [[aspect ratio]] is 2:3, followed by 1:2.

The following flags have a distinctive aspect ratio:

*{{flagof|Belgium|state}}: Aspect ratio of 13:15
*{{flagof|El Salvador}}: Aspect ratio of 189:335
*{{flagof|Monaco}}: Aspect ratio of 4:5
*{{flagof|Nepal}}: The only national flag that is not rectangular, being made with 5 sides, and the only one that is higher than wide. Bordering aspect ratio of ~ 6:5
*{{flagof|Niger}}: Aspect ratio of 6:7
*{{flagof|Qatar}}: The largest aspect ratio of any national flag, the flag's width 2.545 times as large as the height. Aspect ratio of 11:28
*{{flagof|Switzerland}}: (Square-shaped) Aspect ratio of  1:1 
*{{flagof|Togo}}: The [[golden ratio]] which is roughly around 1.618035; Aspect ratio of 2:3.23607 or ~ 8:13
*{{flagof|Vatican City}}: Undefined. The aspect ratio is usually defined around 1:1. However, it is [[Flag of Vatican City#Description|not exactly 1:1]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.protokoll-inland.de/SharedDocs/downloads/Webs/PI/DE/Beflaggung/GenauerBetrachtet/vatikan.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1|access-date = 2021-09-26| language = de|title = letter to the German nunciature (2010-05-27)}}</ref>

This '''armorial of sovereign states''' shows the [[coat of arms]], [[national emblem]], or [[Seal (emblem)|seal]] for every [[list of sovereign states|sovereign state]]. Although some countries do not have an official national emblem, unofficial emblems which are ''de facto'' used as national emblems are also shown below.

Note that due to copyright restrictions in some countries (including [[Arms of Canada|Canada]], [[Coat of arms of South Africa|South Africa]], and [[Coat of arms of Qatar|Qatar]]), some emblems may not be displayed, or may be displayed with slight alterations in appearance from their official rendition, but nonetheless remain faithful to their [[Blazon|heraldic description]].
